How to read this
  • A player counts as returning if she will still have eligibility in the chosen season. Eligibility is estimated from class year (four seasons), with redshirts counted by seasons used.
  • Gap is the depth target minus returning players at that position, minus any commitments you add below. Recruits you haven’t entered aren’t counted, and transfers aren’t modeled.
  • Grad students and 5th-years (marked “?”) are assumed to be out of eligibility. Check these individually.
  • Positions come from school sites as D, M, F and GK only. Players listed at two positions count at the first one; the second shows as a tag and in the “also plays” count.
  • Rosters refresh from each school’s official site every week. Schools marked “Entered by hand” block automated requests, so their rosters are updated manually. Schools not yet loaded are filled in as the weekly refresh reaches them.
